Exact Issue 39

Title: Move source localization out of the model boundary
State: OPEN
URL: https://github.com/warblersafety/wilson-next/issues/39
Comments: none

## Outcome

Move exact source localization out of the model's responsibilities. The model
may propose a normalized fact and the clinician words that support it; Wilson
deterministically associates an exact excerpt with the original input. A
localization failure must remain visible and recoverable rather than displaying
a shifted or truncated quotation.

This is a prerequisite for clinician-facing use, not a prerequisite for
retaining the experimental live-model capability delivered by PR #36.

## Why

Issue #35's final live opening extracted all 29 expected values with correct
product identities, but 22 returned source ranges were systematically shifted
by one character. For example, the intended excerpt `melena and dizziness` was
displayed as ` melena and dizzines`. The immediate code sliced the coordinates
it was given correctly; the bad responsibility split was asking the model to
calculate exact character positions while also interpreting and normalizing
clinical shorthand.

## Minimum scope

- Keep normalized proposed facts separate from verbatim clinician evidence.
- Stop asking the model to calculate character offsets.
- Let deterministic code locate and preserve an exact supporting passage in the
  original input.
- Handle absent or ambiguous matches explicitly without inventing a location or
  silently changing the clinician's words.
- Preserve the rule that a proposal remains unaccepted until human review.
- Add focused synthetic coverage for exact, absent, and ambiguous matches.

## Explicitly excluded

- Fixture-specific offset corrections or an expanding catalog of semantic
  validator rules.
- Fuzzy matching infrastructure unless concrete evidence later shows exact
  matching is insufficient.
- A new persistence layer, diagnostic store, eval framework, provider
  comparison, or retry loop.
- Real clinical data, physician participation, production deployment, or a paid
  plan.

## Acceptance

- Model output no longer contains source character offsets.
- Wilson never presents a shifted, truncated, or reconstructed string as an
  exact clinician quotation.
- Normalized facts may still smooth spelling, abbreviations, dates, units, and
  shorthand when meaning is clear; uncertainty, negation, correction, and
  clinically material distinctions remain preserved.
- A missing or ambiguous source association is shown honestly and cannot
  silently become accepted case knowledge.
- Existing case-authority, correction, conflict, projection, and PDF behavior
  remains intact.

- PR #36
- evidence/issue-35/live-self-contained-evidence/operator-verdict.json
- evidence/issue-35/README.md, section Self-contained-evidence live attempt
