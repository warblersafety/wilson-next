# Explicit D3 treatment duration — issue 181

Fictional Synthetic products A and B only. The current [FDA form](https://www.fda.gov/media/76299/download?attachment=) (09/2025, OMB expiry 09/30/2027) presents D3 dates OR duration. [FDA D3 instructions](https://www.fda.gov/safety/medwatch-forms-fda-safety-reporting/instructions-completing-form-fda-3500) permit estimated duration when dates are unavailable and discuss short therapy. Native time units are minutes, hours, days, weeks, months and years. Instructions also mention dose counts; the native dropdown does not support them. Dose-count, compound/range durations and other unsupported units remain explicit coverage gaps.

The coherent fact retains a supplied positive decimal spelling and unit without date subtraction, unit conversion/default, rounding or inferred amounts. Incomplete amount/unit drafts remain editable across reload; accepted facts require a complete supported pair. Both supplied dates and duration remain in knowledge. When both are known, a clinician-only D3 print choice selects the alternative; missing/unavailable choice has a clear review/clear path. The existing A2 preference UI is reused. No numerical consistency or clinical judgment is attempted. Omission disclosures retain unselected facts/source/qualifier context. Native capacity limits block output instead of truncating amounts.

Independent pypdf traversal in duration-native-appearance.json verifies exact amount strings, both native export IDs, rectangles, options and appearance streams for hours, months and explicit date selection. Native display labels are used to generate appearances before preserving FDA export IDs (6 = hours, 3 = months, 8 = blank). PDFKit rendered pages 4 and 5 of all three PDFs at 2x. All six PNGs were visually inspected: 1.50 Hour(s)/Month(s) appears correctly in both slots; date choice leaves its duration pair blank and preserves the supplied date.
