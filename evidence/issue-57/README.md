# Issue #57 adaptive-completion evidence

This directory is retained synthetic evidence for the bounded adult medication
adverse-event slice. All application-model responses were predetermined. No
live application-model call was made, and Issue #55 was not remediated or tuned
against its failed sample.

## Observed interaction burden

- `adaptive-rich`: two grouped prompts. Accepted indication, weight, two serious
  outcomes, one relevant test, and medical history suppressed duplicate asks;
  the remaining turns confirmed no additional outcomes and collected reporter
  details directly. Duplicate-question count was zero. The reporter block was
  the longest observed turn.
- `adaptive-sparse`: four grouped prompts. The journey recorded indication as
  unknown, no serious outcomes, tests unknown, history declined, and a minimal
  reporter block. None of those answers looped, and truthful partial output
  remained available. Duplicate-question count was zero. Tests and history
  required two explicit dispositions but remained one attributed group.
- The retained Experiment 1 and Experiment 2 journeys also completed under the
  same policy, preserving identity, correction, conflict, browser-state, and
  PDF regressions.

The exact ordered question trace and checkpoint assertions are in
`journey-trace.json`. It records nine predetermined application-model calls and
zero live application-model calls across the assembled suite.

## Browser and PDF agreement

`adaptive-rich-output.png` and `adaptive-sparse-output.png` show the final
browser understanding, omissions, form preview, and download state at a
1440-by-900 Chromium viewport. The rich and sparse PDFs are retained with their
byte lengths and SHA-256 hashes in `pdf-agreement.json`.

The generated bytes were read independently with pypdf 6.16.2. All six PDFs
were unencrypted eight-page documents. Exact named-field assertions covered 45
fields for the adaptive-rich report and 28 for the adaptive-sparse report,
including weight, every serious-outcome checkbox, event date, relevant test and
ranges, history, product data, reporter contact data, occupation, prior-report
targets, and the identity-disclosure control. Sparse omitted values stayed
absent rather than being invented.

`adaptive-rich-pdf-page-{1,2,3,4,7}.png` are independent PDFKit renders of the
retained rich PDF. Manual inspection confirmed that the official FDA/MedWatch
identity and eight-page form layout remain intact and that Sections A, B, D,
and G visually agree with the reviewed browser case. The rendering manifest is
`pdf-rendering-result.json`.
