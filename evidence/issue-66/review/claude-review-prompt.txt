Title: Make supported journeys directly correctable for physician readiness
State: OPEN
URL: https://github.com/warblersafety/wilson-next/issues/66

## Outcome

Make Wilson's existing adult-medication and single-device prototype directly
correctable enough for a meaningful physician-readiness decision. Using
fictional data and predetermined application-model responses, an operator can
detect and correct ordinary misunderstandings or omissions without restarting
the case or asking the model to reinterpret a deterministic field edit. The
reviewed case, visible output, and downloaded Form FDA 3500 remain aligned.

This is a product-completeness slice after the closed Issue #59 campaign. It is
not Layer 4, Layer 5, or remediation of Issue #55.

## Discovery audit on merged baseline

Two existing fictional journeys provide the bounded audit cases: the
information-rich `adaptiveRichOpening` adult medication adverse event and the
information-rich `layer2DeviceOpening` single-device adverse event.

The audit found: opening Change supported only a proposed known scalar and
immediately accepted its group; reviewed facts/report type/reporter facts had
no deterministic edit; supported omitted facts were hidden; proposed tests and
reviewed product/test withdrawal were unsupported; device stopped was hidden;
explicit no-tests appeared as unprovided; and enum literals could reach users.
The existing Fact representation, record-clinician-facts command,
supersession, target validation, projection, and browser state were assessed as
sufficient. Opening multi-correction should use the existing group command.
Accepted-entity withdrawal may add only one small authoritative command and
must not become deletion or a lifecycle framework. No new domain/PDF field was
found necessary.
